How this differs from what already exists

Apolo

Checks freelance delivery evidence before payment. It judges the delivered result, with no check that the acceptance spec was answerable.

MergeProof

Staked review and settlement, scoped to pull requests.

Internet Court

Returns jury verdicts after a dispute has already started. Retainer's whole point is that the dispute is prevented at open time.

GHBounty, Rally

Pay on verified outcomes inside their own verticals.

All of them judge the answer. Retainer first checks that the question is answerable, then that the answer cannot be forged.

Every escrow judges the answer. Two checks come before that.

Both are the reason a dispute does not happen, rather than machinery for resolving one after it has.

Before the brief opens, the spec has to be answerable

A spec that two careful readers read two different ways is not a contract, it is a future dispute. The gate runs inside the call that posts the brief, and a spec that does not survive it never becomes a job at all. No money is locked, and the refusal itself gets a receipt.

If validators cannot agree on whether the spec is answerable, the transaction ends undetermined and the brief does not open. The disagreement is the measurement.

Before the money moves, the verdict has to survive hostile reading

A deliverable that talks the judge into paying it does not get paid. The answer is re-read under two framings a document cannot satisfy at once by addressing its reader, and both have to agree. A round that cannot be read counts against the delivery.

Simulated in your browser by keyword, not by a model and not on chain. It shows the shape of the refusal, never a measurement of one.

Three verdicts, not two

Without a third verdict the entire attack surface collapses to one move: make the deliverable unreadable and get paid anyway. Unverifiable freezes the payment instead of releasing it. Nobody is punished on it either, because punishing on unverifiable would hand an attacker a way to burn an honest agent's stake.

What this page is not claiming

Every number above comes from a Bradbury receipt with a real validator address, or it is not shown at all.

  • Nothing has been measured yet. No figure on this page is presented as a result.
  • The gate and the defence run inside the Retainer contract. Jastrow and Suborn receipts are linked evidence, not cross-contract calls.
  • The settlement rehearsal and the gate console on this page run entirely in the browser. They demonstrate the rules, they do not measure them.
  • The judging prompt is copied byte for byte from Suborn 9c7b0af, which is what the 44-attack corpus measured. The defence framings are adapted and need their own live numbers.
